❄️ Flood-season gate reporting is closed. DWS pauses daily Vaal gate notices over the dry winter (since 19 May 2026). The Vaal is on low / valve discharge — generally safe for bank anglers below the wall. Daily alerts resume with the summer rains. Check DWS directly →

Upper Vaal

Vaal Dam → Parys (~150 km)

Responds to Vaal Dam gate openings within 24–48 hours. Check the Vaal Dam gate notice before fishing the Parys banks.

Middle Vaal

Parys → Vereeniging / Barrage (~180 km)

Affected by Bloemhof releases 3–5 days after discharge. When Bloemhof is releasing over 300 m³/s, bank levels rise significantly.

Lower Vaal

Barrage → Boegoeberg (~400 km)

Barrage controls flow into the lower reach. Controlled releases make this section more predictable — but always check current notices.
